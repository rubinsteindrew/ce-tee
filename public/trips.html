Questions

Asked on the range.

Do I need to be a good golfer?

No. Rounds are played in tournament-style formats with handicaps, so players of different levels can compete. If you can get around eighteen holes at a reasonable pace, you will fit right in.

How do the CE credits work?

Each trip lists its course, instructor and credit hours. Attend the evening sessions and you receive a certificate of completion. Check your state board's rules on continuing education before you book.

What is included in the price?

The trip price covers accommodations, golf, food, beverage and the CE course. Travel to and from the club, and tips for caddies or club staff who go above and beyond, are extra.

Can I reserve more than one spot?

Yes. You can reserve up to four spots at once from your account.

Do I need an account?

Yes. A free account keeps your reservations in one place and lets us send you trip details. You can create one when you reserve.

Is travel included?

No. Each trip card has a "Getting there" note under Day by day, with how far the club is from the airport and when to plan your flights.
