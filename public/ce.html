The CE

Real coursework.Not a slideshow at the bar.

Every trip is built around one continuing education course, taught in person over dinner by an instructor who is on the trip with you. The course and its credit hours are listed before you book.

  1. 01

    One course per trip

    Each trip has a single subject taught start to finish, so every evening builds on the one before.

  2. 02

    Over dinner

    Classes meet in person at the club, over and after dinner, about 2.5 to 3 hours a session. No recordings and no webinars.

  3. 03

    Small groups

    Spots on each trip are limited, so you can ask your question, and you will have played golf with the person answering it.

  4. 04

    Certificate in hand

    Attend each session and you receive a certificate of completion with the course details and credit hours.
