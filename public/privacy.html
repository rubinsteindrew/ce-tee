Privacy policy

Yourinformation.

What CE & Tee collects when you use ce-tee.com, why, and who else handles it. Last updated November 20, 2026.

Who we are

CE & Tee runs golf trips with continuing education courses at ce-tee.com. In this policy, "we" means CE & Tee. Questions about your information go to support@ce-tee.com.

What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your practice name and phone number, if you add them.
  • Your password, stored only as a scrambled hash we can't read back.

When you sign in with Google

Google shares your name, email address, profile photo link and a Google account number with us, and confirms that the email address is yours. We use these only to create your account and sign you in. We don't get access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive, calendar or anything else in your Google account.

When you reserve a trip

  • The trip, how many spots you reserved and the deposit amount.
  • A payment reference from Stripe. Stripe takes your card details on its own page; we never see or store your full card number.

When you browse the site

  • One cookie that keeps you signed in, and a short-lived cookie that protects the Google sign-in step. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • If you turn motion down, your browser remembers that choice on your device. It isn't sent to us.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to run and protect the site.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your trips.
  • To take deposits and keep a record of your reservations.
  • To send emails about your account and trips, such as confirming your address, resetting a password, or details before you travel.
  • To keep the site secure and stop misuse.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

We use a few companies to run the site. Each only gets what it needs for its job:

  • Render hosts the site and stores account and booking records.
  • Stripe processes deposits.
  • Resend delivers our account and trip emails.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google and the fonts on the site.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or with the club and instructor for a trip you've booked, so they can host you.

Information from Google

CE & Tee's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while it's open. If you ask us to delete it, we remove your account details. We keep reservation and payment records as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your choices

  • You can update your name, practice and phone on your account page at any time.
  • Email support@ce-tee.com to get a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account.
  • You can remove CE & Tee's access in your Google account under Security, then Third-party apps.

Security

The site only works over an encrypted connection, passwords are stored as hashes, and sign-in links expire. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

CE & Tee is for licensed professionals. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also email account holders.
