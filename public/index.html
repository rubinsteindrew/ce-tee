What CE & Tees is

Your license needs CE hours. Your golf game needs a trip. CE & Tees puts both on the same itinerary.

  1. Day

    Tournament golf

    Breakfast, golf, lunch, then more golf. Every round is played in a tournament-style format.

  2. Night

    CE courses

    The CE class happens over dinner each evening, taught in person by the trip's instructor.

  3. Home

    Credits earned

    Each trip lists its course and credit hours up front. The price covers your stay, golf, food, beverage and the course. Travel is not included.
